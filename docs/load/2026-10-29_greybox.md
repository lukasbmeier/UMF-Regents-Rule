## Read with it
- The machine is an Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus (18 threads), Windows 11, and it runs SpacetimeDB, the
  six bot processes and the referee at once. A reducer's round trip carries Windows' 15.6 ms timer on the
  server's side (p95 ~15 ms; ~13 ms back to back with nothing else running); the server's own time per
  `submit_input` is 0.05 ms.
- The referee is what breaks (between 150 and 200 bots): it subscribes the whole world, so every bot's
  movement goes through it. The greybox has no terrain, so this is the rows, not Regent's continent.
