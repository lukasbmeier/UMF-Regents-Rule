# AI asset audit (2026-11-14): what Steam's AI disclosure would catch

Luke asked for every 2D, 3D and sound asset that is AI-generated under Steam's AI disclosure, so it can be replaced
with human-made or code-made work. Nothing was changed by the audit. The standing rule that follows from it is in
`GAME.md` (Luke's decisions, "No AI disclosure") and `.claude/rules/regents-genre.md`.

## 1. The policy

Steamworks Content Survey, section on generative AI (rewritten January 2026; check the live form before submitting,
Valve changes it without notice):
- **Pre-Generated**: "Any kind of content that ships with your game and is consumed by players" that "is created
  with the help of AI tools during development". Named kinds: "artwork, sound, narrative, localization, etc."
- **Live-Generated**: content made by AI while the game runs; needs guardrails described. (None here.)
- "Efficiency gains through the use of these tools is not the focus of this section." Coverage of the rewrite reads
  AI-assisted CODE as outside it.
- It covers the store page, capsule art, screenshots, trailers and community assets too, not only the build.

Sources: [Steamworks Content Survey](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey),
[PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/steam/new-gen-ai-disclosure-form),
[The Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/steams-new-ai-policy-separates-ai-generated-content-from-ai-powered-tools-used-for-efficiency-gains/).

## 5. Made by code: fine in this reading

| What | How it is made |
|---|---|
| The ability bar, slots and their marks | Drawn: `draw_arc`, `draw_line`, `draw_circle`, `StyleBoxFlat` (`game/regents/ui/ability_slot.gd`); no image files |
| Menus, windows, the HUD | Built-in Controls and Godot's default font; no image or font files in the repo |
| The minimap and the world map | A top-down camera's picture of the game's own 3D world (`world_map.gd`) |
| The continent's and the expanse's ground textures | Seeded `FastNoiseLite`, tinted (`build_continent.gd`) |
| Item icons (`game/regents/icons/item_*.png`) | Renders of the Quaternius models (`tools/render_icons.tscn`) |
| The minigames' sounds (`game/regents/art/sound/`) | Synthesized by `game/regents/tools/make_skill_sounds.py` |

**The gray area.** An AI wrote those generator scripts and chose their numbers. Read strictly, "with the help of AI
tools" could reach the sounds and the slot marks. The reading here: they are math, not the output of a generative
model trained on others' work, and Valve says tool help is not its focus. Not a promise of how a reviewer reads it.
For zero doubt, Luke tunes the numbers himself, or the disclosure carries one line ("some sound effects and UI marks
are generated by code written with AI assistance").

## 6. Richer art later, without AI

- Bar and HUD: shaders (SDF rings, soft glow, beveled metal from a gradient, cooldown sweeps), `StyleBoxFlat`
  shadows and borders, `NoiseTexture2D` grain.
- Ability icons: rendered from the models like the item icons (a weapon in a pose plus a particle effect), or a
  human-made pack: Kenney (CC0) or game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0: a credit line).
- Sounds: the synth script, or recorded CC0 (freesound, Kenney) noted in the folder's `ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- Gemini mockups (`MINIGAMES_PREP.md` §5.2) stay mockups: none reach the build, the store page or a trailer.
