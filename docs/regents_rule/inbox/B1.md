# B1: THE BAR'S AND TOOLTIPS' WORDS SAY A HIT'S SLOW, SHOVE AND SWEEP
Built:
- `game/regents/ui/ability_bar.gd`: `RegentsAbilityBar.ability_words` adds two lines read from the data, never per
  ability: "It hits every foe in its sweep, not just the first." (`AbilityDef.hits_all`) and, from the kit's `KitHit`
  for that ability (`KitDef.hit_entry`), "Unblocked, it leaves the foe 40% slower and unable to sprint for 3 s, and
  knocks it back at 10 m/s." New helpers: `hit_words(KitHit)`, `status_words(StatusDef)` (from `speed`, `can_act`,
  `can_sprint`, `can_jump`, `can_dash`; the status's name when it changes none of them), `_secs`, `_num`. The staff's
  area and cone spells now say how much their status slows ("leaving those in it 40% slower and unable to sprint for
  3 s") instead of only "slowed". Every caller gets it: the bar's slot tips, the abilities window, the bag's chest
  line (that one passes no kit, so no kit hit is read; a chest spell's own status still shows).
- `game/regents/ui/bar_lab.gd`: a words panel on the right lists every ability of the weapon's set in the same words
  (tooltips do not show in a shot); the shots now include the axe (`bar_axe_0..2.png`).
- `game/regents/tests/suites/regents_bar.gd`: `_run_hit_words` and a lab check (below).
How to play it: hover a Q/E/R slot on the bar, or an ability in the abilities window. Or open
`res://game/regents/ui/bar_lab.tscn` (F6), press Axe: Hamstring Chop, Shoulder Shove and Whirlwind say their slow,
shove and sweep on the right.
