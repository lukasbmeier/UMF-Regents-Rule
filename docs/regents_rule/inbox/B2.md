# B2: BOLT'S FLASH IN THE HAND, METEOR'S RING IN FIRE, OTHERS' MISSILES LIGHTER
Built:
- `game/regents/vfx/ability_vfx_entry.gd`: two new looks on a row of the effects table. `on_cast` is a flash in the
  hand as a spell with no cast time is let go. `ring` is an area spell's own ring.
- `game/regents/vfx/ability_vfx.tres`: a new Bolt row (`on_cast = bolt_cast.tscn`). Meteor's row gets
  `ring = meteor_ring.tscn`.
- `game/regents/vfx/effects/bolt_cast.tscn`: the hand flash. It is a small `VfxParryFlash` in the Bolt's own violet
  (the color `bolt_hit.tscn` uses): 0.45 m, 10 sparks thrown along the shot, 0.3 s, no shake.
- `game/regents/vfx/effects/meteor_ring.tscn`: Meteor's ring. It is a `VfxGroundRing` of the fire element with the
  cinder ring's fire colors and the frost ring's piece count (30). No new effect kind.
- `game/regents/vfx/ability_vfx.gd`: `_cast_flash` plays `on_cast` at `cast_point` (the row's `hand_bone`, `ahead_m`
  in front), facing the aim. Your own plays as you cast (`cast_landing`). Another player's plays from their
  `spell_landed` row while it is fresh (`landed_elsewhere`; the server writes that row for every spell, the Bolt
  too). Never for a spell with a cast time, which shows its charge instead.
- `game/regents/combat/spell_looks.gd`: `ring_scene(ability_id, hostile)` takes the table's `ring` first, otherwise a
  foe's ring in fire and a player's in frost. `show_area` now passes the row's `ability`.
- `game/regents/combat/shot_look.gd`: `make_lighter()` and `_settle_lighter()`. A missile whose body's owner is
  another player (`AbilityVfx.lighter_for`) gets its riding `VfxEffect`s thinned with their lights out, its own sparks
  halved and its light off. This happens from its first frame when the owner is already known (in `_ready`).
  Otherwise it happens once the owner is bound, giving up after 0.5 s. `lighter` set beforehand decides it (the lab).
- `game/regents/vfx/ability_vfx_set.gd`: `release()` lets the cached table go. Before it, the lab closed with "533
  resources still in use", since the new showcases read the table.
- `game/regents/vfx/vfx_lab.gd`: new Bolt and Meteor showcases built from the game's own looks (`bolt_look.tscn`
  flown at 32 m/s; the ring `SpellLooks.ring_scene` picks). O, or `others`, shows another player's.
- `game/regents/tests/suites/regents_vfx.gd`: `_run_bolt_flash`, `_run_meteor_ring`, `_run_shot_lighter`; the lab
  now has 12 showcases.
How to play it: on the staff, left click shows a violet flash in the casting hand as the Bolt leaves. R (Meteor) shows
an orange ring with rising flames where it lands. In two clients, the other player's Bolt flashes in their hand and
its trail is thinner. In the lab (`vfx_lab.tscn`, F6): Bolt and Meteor, with O for another player's.
