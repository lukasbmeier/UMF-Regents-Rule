# Smithing's sounds

Ours: synthesized by `game/regents/tools/make_skill_sounds.py` (no recording, no download), CC0 1.0 (public
domain). `clang` the heavy strike (the minigame pitches it down as the bar cools), `tap` the light tap, `cold` a
strike on a bar too cold to move, `reheat` the bar going back in the fire. Change a number in the script and run it
again to change a sound; a recorded CC0 sound can replace any of them under the same name (note its source here).
